Traditional Chinese (繁體中文) and Simplified Chinese (简体中文) translation for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811432717]Breaking News[/url] by Vel1de.

為 Vel1de 的 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811432717]Breaking News[/url] 提供繁體中文與簡體中文翻譯。

[h2]What is translated[/h2][list]
[*]All event letters and choices (387 keys)
[*]Mod settings and the Breaking News tab, including active effect names and descriptions
[*]Defs: event names, game conditions (superheat, starfall, smokeleaf fog, total darkness, mechanite swarm), earthquake hediff, thoughts, black market trader
[/list]
Terms follow the vanilla Traditional and Simplified Chinese translations. The Simplified Chinese version is converted from the Traditional Chinese one, with game terms replaced by the vanilla Simplified Chinese names.

[h2]翻譯範圍[/h2][list]
[*]所有事件信件與選項（387 條）
[*]模組設定與「突發新聞」分頁，包含作用中效果的名稱與說明
[*]Def：事件名稱、遊戲狀態（超級高溫、流星雨、菸葉煙霧、完全黑暗、機械細胞群）、地震狀態、心情、黑市商人
[/list]
用詞依照原版遊戲的繁體中文翻譯。

[h2]简体中文[/h2]简体中文版由繁体中文版转换而来，游戏用语已替换为原版简体中文的译名（魔鬼素、赫血种、穿梭机、定居点等）。如有用词不自然之处，敬请见谅。

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Load after Breaking News.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[*]One message in the event list ([i]Event can't fire right now.[/i]) is hardcoded in the original mod and stays in English.
[*]Breaking News adds new events often. Text from events added after this translation shows in English until the translation is updated.
[/list]
I made this because I wanted to play Breaking News and no Chinese translation existed yet. If anyone would like to take over the translation, you are welcome to.
